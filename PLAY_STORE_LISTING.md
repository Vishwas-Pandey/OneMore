## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Flying Bird is a classic tap-to-fly arcade game. Guide your bird between torch-lit pillars, timing every flap against gravity — one mistake and it's back to the start, with your best score daring you to beat it.

HOW TO PLAY
- Tap anywhere on screen to flap and rise; let go and gravity pulls you back down.
- Thread the gap between each pair of pillars without touching them, the ground, or the ceiling.
- The pillars close in and speed up the further you get — stay sharp.

FEATURES
- Simple one-tap controls anyone can pick up in seconds
- Escalating difficulty that rewards focus and timing
- Local best-score tracking, so every run is a chance to beat yourself
- Sound toggle
- Optional rewarded video to continue a run in progress
- Pause anytime mid-flight

Flying Bird doesn't ask much of you: just one tap, and the will to try again.
```

(Wording is intentionally plain and literal — no superlative claims.)

## What this listing deliberately avoids, and why

Per the explicit instruction not to make unsupported or misleading claims:
- No "#1 game" / ranking claims — not evaluated against any ranking, so not claimed.
- No "most addictive" / compulsion-framed language — avoided regardless of genre norms.
- No monetization claims beyond what's true — banner/interstitial/rewarded ads run via Google AdMob; the listing only says a rewarded video is "optional," which is accurate (Continue is offered, never forced, and the game is fully playable without ever watching an ad — you just don't get a continue).
- No claims about install counts, ratings, or awards the app does not actually have.
- No use of "Flappy Bird" as a name, and no copying of that game's bird character design — this project deliberately uses an original name and original art to avoid trademark/IP risk, even though the core mechanic (tap-to-flap through gaps) is the same well-established genre.
